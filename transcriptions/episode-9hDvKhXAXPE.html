AI IN HOLLYWOOD: Interview with Mosharaf Chowdhury - AI Energy Expert

Published: October 15, 2026
Dr. Mosharaf Chowdhury is an associate professor of computer science and engineering at the University of Michigan, where he leads SymbioticLab and the ML.ENERGY Initiative, the group behind the ML.ENERGY Leaderboard. He explains to Jessica and Sophie why AI uses energy at all (every token, image and video frame is computation on a GPU), and says his group's recent benchmarking found some video models using far more energy than generating a single image, depending on how long the video is. He gives three reasons AI labs do not publish their energy numbers, explains why running a model locally is not automatically more sustainable than a data center, and says his group has shown large models can be trained with up to 50% less energy with no slowdown or accuracy loss. He also covers Jevons paradox, why he wants apps to show joules per prompt, when a small model is enough, and what Google's August 2025 Gemini energy paper does and does not tell you.
