Published: May 25, 2026
University of Washington Researchers wanted Preschool teachers to wear small wearable cameras to record the classroom to train AI. The research program was "opt-out", meaning parents had to read a form and deny permission. Otherwise, their child would be recorded. The reason for the "opt-out" approach is because if even one parent opts out, the classroom cannot be recorded at all, since it would be impossible to avoid capturing that student on a wearable camera. Rightfully so, people do not trust that any form of data-collecting ensures privacy or protection. Much less, they don't want to be a part of helping feed the AI machine. How will AI world models train on real world situations in sensitive areas like classrooms if we don't want to participate in training?
