AI IN HOLLYWOOD: Interview with Matt Workman - Creative Technologist

Published: October 15, 2026
Matt Workman is a creative technologist at fal, the AI infrastructure company, and a cinematographer who has been posting what he learns about film, game and virtual production technology on YouTube for more than a decade. He explains to Jessica and Sophie what a forward deployed creative technologist does inside studios and game companies, how that role pairs with a newer one called AI enablement, and whether the companies he works with are adopting AI to cut staff. He also covers why he thinks the generalist is back, how employers judge AI filmmakers when the tools are months old, why most of these jobs are filled through networks and personal brand instead of job boards, and what the creator economy and microdramas mean for niche content. He ends with Workman Labs, his site that tracks generative AI job postings, where he says salaries run about four times those at traditional companies.
