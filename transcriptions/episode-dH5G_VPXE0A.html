Are AI watermarks a waste of time and research? #ai #ainews #tech

Published: August 17, 2026
Anthropic recently announced it would be complying with the EU's "AI Act" by watermarking Claude's generated text outputs. And you can't bypass it by "copy/pasting" or manually re-writing the text. It's highly suspected that Anthropic will be using the method developed my Google Deepmind researches called "SynthID" which uses a mechanism called "tournament sampling" to skew the probability of certain generated words in such a way that machine can see a pattern. Some people are arguing that this degrades the quality of the chatbot but researches tested this on users and there was no discernible difference between watermarked and non-watermarked outputs. Even if that's the case, there are still plenty of ways to bypass this type of watermark. So is it worth it?
