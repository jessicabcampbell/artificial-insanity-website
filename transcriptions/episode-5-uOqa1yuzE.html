AI IN HOLLYWOOD: Interview with Marisa X Castro - Line Producer

Published: October 15, 2026
Marisa X Castro is a line producer at Asteria, the AI film studio and lab founded by Bryn Mooser and Natasha Lyonne, with 17 years in animation and VFX and production lead credits on Moana, Frozen and Big Hero 6. She tells Jessica how she plans an animated project that mixes AI with CG or 2D: agreeing with the client up front on tools, legal basis and risk, telling them what percentage of the process is known, running an R&D phase before full production, and reporting spend against the schedule as the work goes. She gives her view on budgets (the $200 million animated feature is no longer palatable, but the target should be $20 to $50 million, not $1 to $3 million), on why a project has to be human-authored and documented from the start if you want to own the IP, and on how roles shift when a visdev artist hands designs to an AI technician who trains a LoRA. She also calls herself an AI skeptic who uses Gemini to polish late emails but not to build schedules or budgets, and talks about the LA job market, the language gap between VC-backed tech and entertainment, who is missing from the rooms where these tools get built, and data centers.
