AI IN HOLLYWOOD: Interview with Andrew McLuhan - Media Philosopher

Published: October 15, 2026
Andrew McLuhan is a media philosopher, the founder and director of The McLuhan Institute, and the grandson of Marshall McLuhan, who coined "the medium is the message." He joins Jessica and Sophie to explain what the phrase means (the medium is the environment a technology creates, and it begins where the content ends) and why he puts AI in the same class as electricity and the internet. He applies his grandfather's and father's ideas to AI: why no tool is "just a tool," why "inevitable" is the wrong word, how jobs are turning into roles, why media ecology calls for global rules, and how the laws of media lead him to describe AI video as an amplification of the imagination. Andrew says the good-or-bad question depends on who you ask, so the better exercise is to write down what you value and check whether AI supports it. He also says slowing AI down makes sense in human terms, that people want connection and depth more than endless automated content, and that every tech company should have a chief media ecologist.
