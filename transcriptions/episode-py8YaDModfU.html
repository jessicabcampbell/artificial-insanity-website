AI IN HOLLYWOOD: Interview with Mowgly Lee - Creative Director

Published: October 15, 2026
Mowgly Lee is creative director at Asteria, the AI film studio and lab founded by Bryn Mooser and Natasha Lyonne, and was on the creative and research team behind Moonvalley's Marey video model. He joins Jessica and Sophie to talk about why Hollywood is heading back to a generalist era, why he says AI saves time more than it saves money, what the "Hollywood is cooked" crowd gets wrong, how Marey was trained on licensed footage instead of scraped data, and what a hybrid AI workflow looks like day to day. He also explains Gaussian splats, the 4D tool he built to change a camera angle after the shot, and where out-of-work filmmakers should start.
